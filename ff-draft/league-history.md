appearances, or a different bracket/side-competition), it was commissioner
braggadocio, or the roll-call email itself was wrong/exaggerated. **Trusting
the Yahoo standings page over the email for the hard fact** (Alex was 2025
champ, 3rd in 2024) — but not deleting the email's claim, since the "why"
behind "back-to-back" is still unresolved. Worth a direct ask to Alex if it
ever matters (e.g. bragging rights get litigated at the draft).
