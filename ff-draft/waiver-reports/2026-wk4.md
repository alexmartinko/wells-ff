# Waiver Report — Week 4 · Allen-ine Fannin Mutants

FAAB: ~$91 of $100 (last confirmed Sept 26 via the standings page — reconfirm
before bidding, a week has passed) · Record 2-0-0 as of that same update.

## The headline, not a generic scan

**Breece Hall (your starting RB2) is week-to-week with a quad injury and not
expected to play Week 4** vs. the Bears (NFL Network/Ian Rapoport, via
multiple outlets). This week isn't "what's trending," it's "replace my own
hurt starter" — that reframes everything below.

## Around the wire

- **RB** — Braelon Allen (NYJ) is the story, see below. Also: Ollie Gordon II
  (MIA), Alvin Kamara (NO, availability unconfirmed — possible OJ already has
  him, his roster is still the one gap in `rosters.md`), Emmett Johnson (KC,
  deeper option).
- **WR** — Dontayvion Wicks (PHI) and Keenan Allen (IND), both below. Also
  Jakobi Meyers (JAX, 7/8 targets Wk3) and Malik Washington (MIA, 50+
  scrimmage yds three straight weeks) as watch-list depth.
- **TE** — Kenyon Sadiq (NYJ) benefits from the same Hall-out game script as
  Allen, and Mike Gesicki (CIN) is still a real streamer, but you don't need
  either — Kincaid + Harold Fannin Jr. covers you.
- **QB** — Geno Smith (NYJ) is a priority add for teams that need one. You
  don't; Allen's entrenched.

## Your moves this week

### 1. Braelon Allen (RB, NYJ) — bid $24, range $15–28

Direct replacement for Hall: presumptive Jets lead back, favorable matchups
(@ Bears Wk4, vs. Browns Wk5). Cross-confirmed independently by RotoBaller
(which ran a dedicated "must-add if Hall misses time" piece), NFL.com, Yahoo,
and NBC Sports — real agreement across outlets, not one site's take.

**Drop: Jonah Coleman** — the Week 3 add that busted (landed on IR without
playing a snap). Dead weight in a bench slot; this swap is the "confirmed
role change" version of the bet that didn't pay off last time.

**Bid logic:** real Week 3 FAAB data (`rosters.md`) shows Kyle is the only
genuinely aggressive bidder in this league, but he's down to **~$27 total**
— so **any bid of $28+ mathematically beats him regardless of what he does.**
Everyone else bids cheap even when active. $24 clears the normal range with
real margin; go to $28+ only if you want the beat-Kyle guarantee.

### 2. Dontayvion Wicks (WR, PHI) — bid $9, range $5–15

Real WR2 role behind DeVonta Smith, 2.8 yd/route run, back-to-back 70+ yard
games (2-73-1, then 5-74-0). Rostered in under half of ESPN leagues — value
the market hasn't caught up to yet. Fixes your eroding bench WR spot.

**Drop: Carnell Tate** — weakest bench WR (11% start), hasn't moved in three
weeks of tracking.

### 3. Keenan Allen (WR, IND) — bid $7, range $4–12

Sizable, consistent role in Indianapolis — a floor play, not a one-week
spike. Deliberately picked over a flashier single-game name after the
Coleman lesson: confirmed role beats a good box score.

**Drop: Rico Dowdle** — already tagged Out, no cost to moving on.

**All three together:** ~$40 of your (last-known) $91 pool.
