# Waiver Report — Week 4 · Allen-ine Fannin Mutants

FAAB: ~$91 of $100 (last confirmed Sept 26 via the standings page — reconfirm
before bidding, a week has passed) · Record 2-0-0 as of that same update.

## The headline, not a generic scan

**Breece Hall (your starting RB2) is week-to-week with a quad injury and not
expected to play Week 4** vs. the Bears (NFL Network/Ian Rapoport, via
multiple outlets). This week isn't "what's trending," it's "replace my own
hurt starter" — that reframes everything below.

## Around the wire

- **RB** — Braelon Allen (NYJ) is the story, see below. Also: Ollie Gordon II
  (MIA), Alvin Kamara (NO, availability unconfirmed — possible OJ already has
  him, his roster is still the one gap in `rosters.md`), Emmett Johnson (KC,
  deeper option).
- **WR** — Dontayvion Wicks (PHI) and Jordan Addison (MIN), both below.
  Courtland Sutton (DEN) looked good by the numbers but is ruled out — see
  "Corrected from v1." Also Jakobi Meyers (JAX, 7/8 targets Wk3) and Malik
  Washington (MIA, 50+ scrimmage yds three straight weeks) as watch-list
  depth.
- **TE** — Kenyon Sadiq (NYJ) benefits from the same Hall-out game script as
  Allen, and Mike Gesicki (CIN) is still a real streamer, but you don't need
  either — Kincaid + Harold Fannin Jr. covers you.
- **QB** — Geno Smith (NYJ) is a priority add for teams that need one. You
  don't; Allen's entrenched.

## Your moves this week

### 1. Braelon Allen (RB, NYJ) — bid $24, range $15–28

Direct replacement for Hall: presumptive Jets lead back, favorable matchups
(@ Bears Wk4, vs. Browns Wk5). Cross-confirmed independently by RotoBaller
(which ran a dedicated "must-add if Hall misses time" piece), NFL.com, Yahoo,
and NBC Sports — real agreement across outlets, not one site's take.

**Drop: Jonah Coleman** — the Week 3 add that busted (landed on IR without
playing a snap). Dead weight in a bench slot; this swap is the "confirmed
role change" version of the bet that didn't pay off last time.

**Bid logic:** real Week 3 FAAB data (`rosters.md`) shows Kyle is the only
genuinely aggressive bidder in this league, but he's down to **~$27 total**
— so **any bid of $28+ mathematically beats him regardless of what he does.**
Everyone else bids cheap even when active. $24 clears the normal range with
real margin; go to $28+ only if you want the beat-Kyle guarantee.

### 2. Dontayvion Wicks (WR, PHI) — bid $9, range $5–15

Real WR2 role behind DeVonta Smith, 2.8 yd/route run, back-to-back 70+ yard
games (2-73-1, then 5-74-0). Rostered in under half of ESPN leagues — value
the market hasn't caught up to yet. Fixes your eroding bench WR spot.

**Drop: Carnell Tate** — weakest bench WR (11% start), hasn't moved in three
weeks of tracking.

### 3. Jordan Addison (WR, MIN) — bid $13, range $8–18

**Revised pick** — see "Corrected from v1" below for what this replaced and
why. Locked in as Minnesota's No. 2 WR (17.4% target share) and already
proved he produces with a bigger role: 5-9-90-1 TD in Week 3 after Justin
Jefferson left with an ankle sprain. Jefferson is questionable for Week 4
("could play," monitored through practice all week per NFL Network) — if he
sits, Addison is a real spike play; if he plays, Addison's still a
productive, rostered No. 2. Real expert consensus (not a rank I couldn't
verify) has him **WR27** for Week 4 — genuinely flex-relevant.

**Drop: Rico Dowdle** — already tagged Out, no cost to moving on.

**All three together:** ~$46 of your (last-known) $91 pool.

## Corrected from v1 — vetted with real research, not just projections

Alex pushed back on the original #3 (Keenan Allen) after seeing a live Yahoo
free-agent screen with better-projected names sitting right there. Went
back and actually researched each candidate instead of trusting either the
raw projection number or my own earlier dismissal:

- **Courtland Sutton (WR, DEN) — ruled OUT despite a good projection
  (10.85).** Denver traded for Jaylen Waddle, who now starts ahead of him;
  Sutton's been bumped to WR2, hasn't scored all season, and did "little
  with three catches" last week. This is exactly the trap: a good number
  sitting on top of a bad situation the projection hasn't caught up to.
- **RJ Harvey (RB, DEN) — checked, not worth a bid.** Real role, but it's
  third-down/passing-work only; Dobbins still owns the rushing load. You
  already have Kyren + Rhamondre covering that same passing-down role on
  your own roster — Harvey doesn't add anything Allen doesn't already cover
  better.
- **Jordan Addison — reinstated.** Two weeks ago I dismissed him on a
  specific ranking (WR38) that a parallel session later confirmed was
  fabricated (see Process note below and the Week 2 retraction). That bad
  call quietly carried forward until Alex's screenshot forced a fresh look.
  Real research this time: locked WR2 role, real Week 3 production, a
  genuine Jefferson-injury contingency, and a verifiable WR27 consensus
  rank. Replaces Keenan Allen, whose case ("steady role") was weaker than
  Addison's once actually compared side by side.
- Also flagged from that screenshot: Wan'Dale Robinson and Quentin Johnston
  showed as available even though `rosters.md` had them on Kyle's and Zach's
  benches as of Sept 26 — likely dropped since, file is stale. Needs a fresh
  roster paste; not chasing either one without more research regardless.
