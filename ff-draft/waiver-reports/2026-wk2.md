## Your moves this week

### 1. Add Jalen Coker (WR, CAR) — bid $20–24

8 tgt / 8 rec / 138 yd / 2 TD in the Week 1 opener, and he outright led
Carolina's offense — not a garbage-time line, a real role in an offense that
now knows what it has. Directly upgrades your worst roster spot: bench WRs
Marvin Harrison Jr. (24% start) and Carnell Tate (18% start) are both
low-confidence flex plays, and Coker looks better than either right now.

Bid aggressively on purpose: Joe, Hai, and Kyle all manage waivers hard in
this league, and a 2-TD debut is exactly the kind of name they chase too.
Early season with a full $100 pool is the time to pay up for a real role
rather than get outbid saving pennies.

**Drop: Carnell Tate** — weakest bench spot (8.81 proj, buried in a crowded
TEN receiving corps), clears the roster spot.

### 2. Speculative — Kendre Miller (RB, NO) — bid $2–4

Alvin Kamara was inactive Week 1, and Miller stepped into a real rotation:
tied Travis Etienne Jr. for the team lead in carries (9), 26 snaps (a career
high), a TD. If Kamara's absence continues even part-time, Miller's a
flex-relevant handcuff-with-upside, not just a lottery ticket.

Not urgent — you're not desperate at RB (4 rostered already) — but Chuba
Hubbard is your shakiest piece there (37% start), and this is a cheap way to
buy insurance without touching real budget. Low competition expected: RB
isn't a need for Joe/Hai/Kyle's rosters either, so this should go for near
the minimum.

**No drop needed if you carry 17 bench spots open** — otherwise Bo Nix
(QB2, redundant behind Allen) is the easiest cut.
