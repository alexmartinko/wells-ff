## The actual question: should Alex bid on Cam Skattebo (RB, NYG)?

**No — pass, or a $1-2 token dart at most, not a real bid.** This is exactly
the trap the house process exists to catch: Skattebo shows up on this
league's free-agent board with a gaudy preseason rank (70th overall) that's
collapsed to **752nd actual** and he's still **93% rostered** across Yahoo
at large — the kind of stat line that looks like a buy-low smash. It isn't.

- **It's not an injury.** His 2025 season-ending ankle dislocation/fibula
  fracture is fully healed — he was a full camp participant in 2026 and
  played through Week 6. The crater is **performance/trust-driven**: he
  fumbled four times by Week 5, including a costly overtime fumble in the
  Week 5 tie with Washington that killed a potential game-winning
  field-goal drive. In Week 6 vs. New Orleans he got **zero touches** —
  Harbaugh denied it was a formal benching, but admitted "when he was in
  there, we didn't call any runs." That's a demotion in everything but
  name. ([RotoBaller — zero touches](https://www.rotoballer.com/player-news/cam-skattebo-gets-zero-touches-in-loss/1969715),
  [RotoBaller — Harbaugh denies benching](https://www.rotoballer.com/player-news/harbaugh-says-cam-skattebo-was-not-benched-in-week-6/1970134))
- **Tyrone Tracy Jr. is the clear beneficiary** of the fumble-driven
  shakeup — the backfield has shifted to him, not back to Skattebo.
- **The Giants are on a bye in Week 8** (confirmed on the free-agent board —
  Skattebo's line shows "Bye," no game). Zero urgency to roster him for
  this week even as a pure speculative stash.
- The 93% Ros / high preseason pedigree is a lagging, not a quality,
  signal — most other Yahoo managers haven't processed the demotion yet.
  Same pattern this project has gotten burned by before (Fidone, Sutton).

If Harbaugh hands the backfield back to him post-bye, that's a Week 9
story to revisit with a fresh snap-count/touch report — not something to
pay for blind this week, especially with better immediate options below
and limited bench space.

## Your moves this week

### 1. Jordan Mason (RB, MIN) — bid $12, range $8–16

Mason was activated off IR (thumb) for Week 7 and **immediately led the
Vikings in rushing** (15 carries, 68 yards) after Aaron Jones re-aggravated
a prior knee injury and left the game. ([Fantasy Footballers — leading
rusher in return](https://www.thefantasyfootballers.com/news/644353/jordan-mason-leading-rusher-in-return),
[Fantasy Footballers — Jones aggravates knee](https://www.thefantasyfootballers.com/news/644317/aaron-jones-aggravates-previous-knee-injury))
Jones's workload is now genuinely in question going forward, and even in a
timeshare Mason is a legitimate FLEX piece on volume (11.37 fantasy pts
last week, actual rank 82 overall per the board). This directly fills
Alex's real gap: with Breece Hall on IR, **Alex has zero bench RB depth
right now** — Kyren, Rhamondre, and Hubbard are all starting with no one
behind them.

**Drop: Wan'Dale Robinson (WR, TEN).** Picked up off Kyle's cut since the
last roster snapshot; lowest-value, lowest-target-share piece on the bench
(4.70 pts last week, 19% start rate) and the clearest cut given the actual
need is at RB, not a 6th-7th WR.

### 2. Rachaad White (RB, WAS) — bid $14, range $10–20

Washington placed starter **Jacory Croskey-Merritt on IR (groin, re-injury)
for a minimum of four games** in late October — he'd been Washington's
leading rusher (67 carries, 239 yards, 2 TD) before the injury. White is
now the lead back in the timeshare that's left (with Austin Ekeler a clear
second option, not a threat — Ekeler's actual rank has fallen to 240).
([Newsweek/TSN — Croskey-Merritt to IR](https://www.tsn.ca/nfl/article/commanders-place-starting-rb-croskey-merritt-groin-on-ir-n1-50221053/),
[DraftSharks — White grabs Commanders lead role, 2nd RB1 week](https://www.draftsharks.com/fantasy-football-news/83585/white-grabs-commanders-lead-role-and-delivers-his-second-rb1-week))
White's actual-points rank has jumped from 182nd preseason to 73rd,
backed by two strong weeks, not one fluky game — 12.23 fantasy points
last week on real volume (13.5 carries, 55.4 yards, 2.9 targets).

**Drop: C.J. Stroud (QB, HOU).** Stroud was a one-week streamer for Josh
Allen's Week 7 bye — Allen's bye has passed, he reclaims the starting QB
job this week, and carrying two startable QBs in a single-QB format is a
luxury Alex can't afford with the roster needing RB help. (Not the "cut a
talented player over a short injury" mistake flagged in `2026-wk6.md` —
Stroud is healthy and productive, this is a pure positional-surplus cut.)

**Also do, not a FAAB move:** swap Josh Allen back into the starting QB
slot for Week 8 now that his bye has passed — the roster screenshot still
has Stroud starting from last week's bye-week lineup.
