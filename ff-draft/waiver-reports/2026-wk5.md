# Waiver Report — Week 5 · Allen-ine Fannin Mutants

FAAB: $84 of $100 (live, Oct 6 standings page) · Record 4-0-0, 1st place,
best points-for in the league. Filed as "Week 5" from the game dates in the
screenshots (10/7 onward, 4 games already played) — Alex's message called
it "week 4," likely informal; this is the period right after `2026-wk4.md`.

## The real crisis: RB, not a soft spot anymore

Of 4 rostered RBs, only **Kyren Williams and Rhamondre Stevenson are
healthy and playing**. Breece Hall is still Out (quad/thigh from Week 3, no
return timeline — the Jets are leaning on a committee, not a set date).
Rico Dowdle is Out. Chuba Hubbard is on bye this week. That's 3 of 5 bench
spots currently dead weight.

## Ruled out despite good numbers on the live Yahoo screen

- **Thomas Fidone II (TE, NYG)** — top of the available list by projection
  (14.43, +76.6% trend). Real research: **healthy scratch for all 4 games**,
  buried behind Theo Johnson and newly-signed Isaiah Likely. The projection
  is noise; he's not active. Same trap as Sutton weeks ago — don't trust the
  number without checking the depth chart.
- **Courtland Sutton (WR, DEN)** — reconfirmed still bad. 9 catches, 86
  yards, zero TDs through 4 games; Jaylen Waddle has fully taken the WR1
  role. Pass, consistent with the Week 4 call.

## Your moves this week

### 1. Will Shipley (RB, PHI) — bid $35, range $20–45+

**Both Eagles backs ahead of him are out.** Saquon Barkley (hamstring)
unlikely to play; Tank Bigsby needs core muscle surgery and is going to IR.
Cross-verified independently by RotoBaller (two separate dedicated
articles), FantasyPros, Bleacher Report, and a Yahoo FAAB report naming him
specifically. The cleanest, most confirmed opportunity on the board — fixes
the RB crisis directly. Only 1-2% rostered historically, but this is
genuine league-wide headline news this week, not an under-the-radar name —
expect more competition than past picks even though this league's bidders
have mostly been cheap so far.

**Drop: Rico Dowdle** — Out for weeks, no path back to relevance.

### 2. Dohnte Meyers (WR, CIN) — bid $15, range $8–22

Real breakout: 7 catches, 82 yards on 9 targets after Ja'Marr Chase left
Week 4 with a concussion (still in protocol). Ran with the 1st-team offense
in practice this week per the Bengals' WR coach. Not a random flier — **Tee
Higgins (your own starter) is day-to-day with an adductor strain**, so this
is direct insurance on your own offense, not just a league-wide trend chase.

**Drop: Keenan Allen** — just went Out, no longer contributing.

### 3. Braelon Allen (RB, NYJ) — bid $18, range $10–26

Still Breece Hall's direct replacement — Hall remains out with no
timeline, Jets "leaning on Allen and a committee approach." Less of a clean
takeover than hoped a few weeks ago, but you're down to 2 healthy RBs, so
real depth still matters here regardless.

**Drop/decision:** this is the one real judgment call, not a default —
**recommend dropping Breece Hall himself.** You're about to roster his own
direct replacement; holding an also-hurt player with no return date while
also carrying Allen is low value right when there are 3 real adds to make.
Alternative: skip this pick, stash Hall through his return, keep the bid
money. Alex's call.

**All three together: ~$68 of your $84.** If that's too much to commit in
one week, Shipley is the one not to miss; Meyers and Allen are good, not
irreplaceable.

## Why bigger bids than past weeks

Shipley specifically is a genuine industry-wide headline (not under-the-
radar like Coker/Wicks were), so the league's historical cheap-bidding
pattern is less predictive this time — more managers than usual are likely
to recognize the name. Kyle remains capped at **$24 total** (barely moved
from $27 two weeks ago) — any bid **$25+ on anything mathematically beats
him** regardless of what he does.
