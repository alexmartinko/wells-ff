# Waiver Report — Week 6 · Allen-ine Fannin Mutants

FAAB: $76 of $100 (live, Oct 14 standings page) · Record 4-1-0, 2nd place
on a tiebreak behind Hai, best-or-near-best points-for in the league all
season. Filed from the live app screenshots — games for this period start
10/14 onward (Thursday Seahawks game first).

## Since last week: one of two recommendations landed

`2026-wk5.md` recommended two moves. Only one happened:
- **Dohnte Meyers (WR, CIN) — added**, ~$8 FAAB spent, right at the low end
  of the suggested $8-22 range. Rico Dowdle was the actual drop (not Keenan
  Allen as suggested) — a better call than the plan, since Keenan Allen
  cleared his injury and is back starting this week.
- **Will Shipley (RB, PHI) — not added.** Turned out to be the right call
  independent of the reasoning at the time: Shipley suffered a partial
  plantar fascia tear in Week 5 and is now out "multiple weeks" himself,
  so the roster spot wasn't wasted on a backfield that's since cratered
  further (Barkley hamstring, Bigsby on IR, now Shipley hurt too — Eagles
  are down to Dameon Pierce/Mannion/practice-squad bodies).

## Roster notes, not moves

- **Marvin Harrison Jr. was traded from Arizona to Houston** (Oct 11,
  2026) — already on Alex's bench, nothing to do, but it's a real
  stock-up. Houston gave up a 2027 second-rounder; the expectation per
  ESPN/Bleacher Report is Harrison slots in as Houston's No. 2 behind Nico
  Collins, reunited with college QB C.J. Stroud. Worth stashing through
  the adjustment period rather than cutting now.
- **Breece Hall (quad) is tagged Q, not Out, for the first time since Week
  3** — trending back. Keep holding; this is the same "don't cut a
  talented player over a short injury" lesson from the Week 5 retraction.
- **Tee Higgins and Dohnte Meyers are both on bye** (Cincinnati) this week
  — not injuries, just the bye. Both Ja'Marr Chase and Higgins have
  cleared their earlier injuries, so Meyers' direct-insurance rationale
  from last week (filling in for an injured Higgins) is now moot — see
  below, this makes him the cleanest drop candidate if a move is needed.

## Your move this week

### Woody Marks (RB, HOU) — bid $18, range $10–30

**Houston has handed him the backfield.** David Montgomery (groin) is
"week-to-week," not a one-game ding, and Marks just posted his best game
of the season in relief (62 yards on 15 carries, a TD called back by
penalty) plus real receiving work (season line through Week 6: 149 rush
yards on 43 carries, 11 catches for 54 yards on 13 targets). Multiple
independent sources confirm the role change, not just a single hot
projection: Yahoo Sports ("Texans turn to Woody Marks as new RB1"),
NBC Sports, and profootballrumors.com all have him as Houston's lead back
with Montgomery out. This directly addresses Alex's actual roster
gap — even with Hall trending back and Hubbard active, this bench is one
injury from thin again, and Marks is a real three-down option, not a
touchdown-dependent flier like last week's pickups.

**Drop: Dohnte Meyers.** His whole rationale last week was "insurance for
an injured Higgins" — both Chase and Higgins are healthy again (just on
a bye), so Meyers goes back to being a buried WR3/4 in a crowded, healthy
Cincinnati receiving corps. Cleanest cut on the roster; nothing else
(Harrison stock-up, Hall/Higgins on the "don't cut talent over a short
absence" list) should go instead.

**Competition read:** real per-owner FAAB data (see `rosters.md`'s Oct 14
table) still shows this is a cheap-bidding league outside of one capped-out
team — Kyle is stuck at $24 (any bid $25+ mathematically beats him
regardless), and **Steph and Zach are still sitting on untouched $100
budgets after 5 games**, a real pattern now, not noise. Hai ($93), Sara
($99), and OJ ($99) also didn't move a dollar in the last week. The one
risk: Marks is a genuine national headline (multiple major-site writeups,
not an under-the-radar name), so expect more bites than a typical week —
sizing the bid toward the middle of the range rather than the floor.
